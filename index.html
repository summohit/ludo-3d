      📜 Ludo 3D Arena Rules

      • Roll a 6 to Launch: Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of your Home Yard onto your colored Start square.
      • Bonus Rolls: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent's pawn, or reaching the Center Home Crown grants an instant Bonus Roll! (Three 6s in a row forfeits your turn).
      • Safe Star Shields (★): Pawns standing on any colored Start tile or Star ★ tile are protected and cannot be captured.
      • Knockout Captures: Landing on an opponent's pawn on a normal tile blasts it back to their Home Yard in 3D!
      • Win Condition: First player to bring all 4 pawns into the Center Home Crown wins!
